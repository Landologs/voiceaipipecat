# Sources and provenance

The initial entries are short, original descriptions assembled for this project. No dictionary, corpus, FAQ, or commercial glossary was scraped or reproduced wholesale.

## Terminology references

- [Academy of the Hebrew Language terminology: plumbing](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/25776_2/plumbing) — consulted to verify `שרברבות` as the formal Hebrew term for plumbing.
- [Academy terminology: drain pipe](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/25757_2/drain%20pipe) — consulted for `נקז` and `צינור ניקוז`.
- [Academy terminology: drain valve](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/25671_2/drain%20valve) — consulted for valve and drainage terminology.
- [Israel Water Authority public FAQ](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/qa_pniyot) — consulted for ordinary descriptions associated with visible leaks, faucet dripping, cistern leakage, wall dampness, and water-meter movement. The dataset does not reproduce the FAQ's instructions.

## Spoken Hebrew references

- [Hebrew Wiktionary: סבבה](https://he.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%A1%D7%91%D7%91%D7%94), [אחלה](https://he.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%90%D7%97%D7%9C%D7%94), [חבל על הזמן](https://he.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%91%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%9C_%D7%94%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%9F), and [באסה](https://he.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%91%D7%90%D7%A1%D7%94) — consulted to verify meanings and ambiguity of a few common expressions.

Hebrew Wiktionary text is offered under CC BY-SA 4.0. We record attribution here and use independently written, minimal meanings and variants rather than copying entries. The Academy and government pages were used as factual terminology references; their prose is not included. Russian and English entries use ordinary generic trade vocabulary and original project definitions.

## Provider capability references

- [OpenAI GPT-Live-Transcribe](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-live-transcribe) — documents streaming transcription plus multiple language hints.
- [OpenAI GPT-4o Mini TTS](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-4o-mini-tts) and [text-to-speech guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/text-to-speech) — document the current speech model, supported languages, voice parameter, and the warning that built-in voices are optimized for English.

The provider pages establish API capabilities, not equal pronunciation quality across languages. Hebrew, Russian, and English voice quality still needs live listening tests with the selected voice.
